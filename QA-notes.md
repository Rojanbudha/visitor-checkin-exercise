**1. Highest-Risk Area**

The highest-risk area for me is the deactivation feature.

Reasoning: It is clearly stated in the feature overview that deactivated visitors must not appear in the active list or be selectable for repeat visits. But after testing it we confirmed that both the statement doesn't hold true. A deactivated visitor still appears in the active visitors list as i mention in the (TC-10) and is still returned by search as in (TC-11). The main purpose of this feature to block someone or something before anything happen that might threaten the system but it drastically fails to do. Also there is no UI button at frontend part to trigger deactivation which is annoying.

**Question for the Product Owner**

What is the main purpose of deactivation here, is it to block future registration or block someone who is still checked in?
